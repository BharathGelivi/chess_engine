Overboard: Free Chess Engine & Analysis Board

Overboard is a free chess engine and analysis board that runs entirely in your browser. Import a game as a PGN and step through it move by move, or play live, while getting move suggestions from Stockfish 18 or Overboard Engine, a from-scratch chess engine built for this project.

Free chess engine analysis

Get up to five suggested moves per position, an evaluation bar, and a material-balance readout as you browse any position in your game.

Self chess play — engine vs engine

Watch two chess engines play each other automatically in self chess play mode, in any combination of Stockfish 18 and Overboard Engine.
